# PTCGL Replays

A web app to watch replays from battle logs.

# How it works

- After you finish a game, copy your battle log.
- Go to the [PTCGL Replays](https://ptcgl-replays.vercel.app/) website and paste your battle log into the input field.
- The app will parse the battle log and display the replay of the game, allowing you to watch the moves and actions taken by both players.

# Features

- Save and share your replays
- Browse publicly available replays
